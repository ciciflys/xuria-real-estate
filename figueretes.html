Investment Thesis

Four reasons this asset works.

A turnkey, remote-managed apartment with a regulatory tailwind and a low-headache operating model — built for owners who want returns without on-island presence.

01

Turnkey from day one.

Fully furnished apartment: sofa, dining for four, complete kitchen with washer-dryer combo, dishwasher, oven, microwave; three double beds (one a Murphy bed that converts to a desk); terrace furniture and decor throughout. Established cleaning vendor (€150 / turnover) and Murphy pickup delivery (€85 / turnover) continue with new ownership.

02

Remote operations.

Smart keypad check-in lets the new owner operate from anywhere in the world. Existing systems already proven over multiple seasons. No on-island presence required.

03

Regulatory tailwind.

Operates under Spain's seasonal-rental regime (32-night minimum, governed by Spanish Urban Lease Law). Fully compliant with current Balearic regulation. New owner can re-register the National Rental Registration (NRA) at closing — a straightforward administrative process.

04

Lower-headache operating model.

50-night average guest stays mean roughly 6 turnovers per year, vs. the 30–50 of a nightly let. Higher-quality long-stay tenants — executives, remote-working families — choose months, not weekends. Lower wear, fewer cleaning cycles, predictable cashflow.
