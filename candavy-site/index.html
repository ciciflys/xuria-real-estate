The Villa

A refined interpretation of conscious luxury.

Can Davy blends clean, open-plan architecture with the warmth of organic materials and high-end amenities — solar-powered, energy-efficient, and thoughtfully designed throughout. Set on a peaceful residential street minutes from Ibiza Town, it offers rare walkable access to the beach, dining and shops. Expansive glass doors dissolve the line between inside and out, opening to a shaded terrace and saltwater pool, while the rooftop above takes in views over the sea and Dalt Vila — a private sanctuary of style, wellness and ease, for six.
